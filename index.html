ABOUT ME

I'm evavic, a software architect drawn to the parts of a product that have to work when traffic grows or an external service sends incomplete data. I work across backend APIs, databases, and cloud infrastructure. When choosing a design, I think about who will maintain it, what they'll need to see when something fails, and how easily they can change it later.

I'm optimistic and persistent when a problem gets messy. If a bug only shows up occasionally, I start with what I can reproduce, break the problem into smaller pieces, and test one explanation at a time. I enjoy finding patterns, spotting assumptions that don't hold, and explaining what I learned so someone else can pick up where I left off. That mix of curiosity and logical thinking is why programming feels like a natural fit for me.

China Post Tracker reflects that approach. A shopper may start with a tracking number and a single question about an order. The site puts the latest available shipment updates alongside guides that explain unfamiliar statuses, delayed scans, and final delivery.

I'm exploring related problems across online shopping and e-commerce, from making carrier events easier for developers to use to helping merchants spot delayed orders and follow returns through to a refund.
