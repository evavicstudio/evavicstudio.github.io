Work

Featured project

  • China Post Tracker

    Enter a tracking number to look up shipping updates for China Post, EMS, and ePacket parcels. The site also explains tracking statuses and offers guidance on stalled updates, customs, and final delivery.

    Open China Post Tracker

Project concepts

  • Dayboard

    A daily planner that pairs a task list with time blocks so you can see what fits before filling your schedule.

  • Pantry Notes

    A kitchen inventory organized by use-by date, with a shopping list for staples that are running low.

  • Pocket Ledger

    A budget tracker that groups expenses by category and shows what remains after upcoming bills.

  • Trail Notebook

    A field notebook for logging walks, recording route conditions, and saving places worth revisiting.

  • Shelf Queue

    A reading list that tracks borrowed books, due dates, and what you want to read next.

  • Neighborhood Noticeboard

    A local noticeboard for community events and announcements, with filters by date and neighborhood.

  • Repair Log

    A home maintenance journal that keeps service dates, replacement parts, and follow-up tasks with each appliance.
